# DSAI Market Research Notes

## Official sources reviewed

NIST describes the AI Risk Management Framework as a voluntary framework for incorporating trustworthiness into the design, development, use, and evaluation of AI products, services, and systems. The current NIST page also points to the Generative AI Profile and organizes the risk-management orientation around govern, map, measure, and manage.

Source: [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)

UNESCO’s guidance for generative AI in education and research calls for a human-centred approach, protection of data privacy, age-appropriate use, ethical validation, and pedagogical design. This is relevant to DSAI’s Bengali student and professional workflows: education features should assist learning and review rather than silently replace human judgment.

Source: [UNESCO Guidance for generative AI in education and research](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research)

OpenAI’s Projects guidance describes persistent spaces that keep chats, files, instructions, and context together for ongoing work. This confirms that project-level context is a current user expectation, not a sufficient category moat by itself.

Source: [OpenAI Academy: Using projects in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/academy/projects/)

GitHub’s Copilot product page emphasizes AI across multiple surfaces, custom agents, project knowledge, enterprise governance, audit logs, and MCP access control. The implication for DSAI is that an AI system becomes more valuable when it provides a control plane and trusted workflow around models, not only a chat box.

Source: [GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot)

## Strategic implications

DSAI should position itself as a **Bengali-first, evidence-led Workdesk** for converting mixed-language requests and documents into reviewable Work Packets. The moat should be built from Bengali/Banglish workflow taxonomy, consented evaluation data, source/evidence provenance, human approval records, privacy controls, and operator playbooks. It should not rely on a claim of owning a unique visual style or on generic access to foundation models.

The highest-value AI roles are: intent router, packet planner, evidence and uncertainty assistant, Bengali language normalizer, safety and privacy gatekeeper, reviewer copilot, and evaluation analyst. Each role needs a bounded contract, user-visible status, fallback behavior, and a measurable outcome.

The operating model should use a three-speed loop: fast public education and examples, controlled alpha Work Packets with human review, and provider-gated automation only after consent, auditability, retries, and rollback are demonstrated. Key metrics should include time-to-useful-draft, correction rate, source coverage, review turnaround, export/delete success, mobile task completion, Bengali/Banglish quality, provider failure recovery, and paid retention.

## Claims discipline

No source proves that DSAI is globally unmatched. The defensible claim is narrower: DSAI is building a Bengali-first Workdesk that combines structured packets, evidence, human gates, privacy controls, and public capability evidence. Market leadership must be earned through user outcomes and transparent longitudinal evaluation.
