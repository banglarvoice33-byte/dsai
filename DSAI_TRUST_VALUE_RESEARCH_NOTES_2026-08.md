# DSAI Trust, Value, and Public Answer Research Notes

## Authoritative findings

NIST describes the AI Risk Management Framework as a voluntary framework to incorporate trustworthiness into the design, development, use, and evaluation of AI products and systems. Its lifecycle emphasis supports DSAI’s existing Govern/Map/Measure/Manage approach, with additional attention to validity, reliability, safety, security, resilience, accountability, transparency, explainability, privacy, and fairness. NIST also publishes a Generative AI Profile for risks specific to generative systems. [1]

Google Search guidance states that generative AI can help research and structure original content, but generating many pages without adding value may violate scaled-content-abuse policy. Public content should prioritize accuracy, quality, and relevance, and automated content should give users context about how it was created where appropriate. [2]

The OECD AI Principles describe trustworthy AI as human-centred and rights-respecting. They emphasize transparency and explainability, human agency and oversight, robustness, security, safety, accountability, traceability across the AI lifecycle, and the ability to challenge adverse outputs. These principles support DSAI’s use of durable traces, approval gates, evidence fields, redacted telemetry, provider boundaries, and public limitation statements. [3]

## Implications for DSAI

The strongest long-term value is not a claim of being the most powerful general model. It is a Bengali-first, evidence-aware Workdesk that makes complex work reviewable, bounded, and accountable. Defensible value should be demonstrated through repeatable workflows, measurable Bengali quality, traceability, safe file handling, human review, integration boundaries, and observable user outcomes.

Public answers should be written as product facts with status labels: implemented, preview, provider-gated, or roadmap. Search-facing content should answer user intent directly, add original evidence and useful context, avoid scaled generic pages, and disclose automation where it helps understanding. Investor or shareholder messaging should focus on evidence, adoption hypotheses, unit economics, retention, quality gates, and risk controls rather than guaranteed growth or returns.
