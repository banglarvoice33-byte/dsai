# DSAI maximum capability and value review

**Status:** Controlled Alpha / deployable preview. This review separates what the project can demonstrate now from what still requires external provider, merchant, VPS, legal or field evidence.

## Executive assessment

DSAI’s highest verified value is not an unsupported promise of an autonomous super-agent. It is a Bengali-first operational Workdesk that converts complex input into structured, evidence-aware next steps, preview-first work packets, quality gates, traceable agent simulations, capability boundaries, and controlled creative planning. The strongest near-term commercial wedge is a lead-response and workflow-readiness service: fast acknowledgement and qualification, human-reviewed action packets, read-only integration pilots, and an operator-safe path to deployment.

The current project can credibly support a public preview, product discovery, workflow planning, quality/evidence review, admin observability, capability education, and controlled-alpha onboarding. It cannot yet claim live Visa/bKash/Nagad/Rocket charging, real CRM/calendar write actions, unrestricted autonomous outreach, real media-provider execution, custom-domain production health, or verified three-second end-to-end meeting confirmation.

## Highest-return project-side moves

The project-side priorities are a transparent Plans/Upgrade surface, a provider-neutral payment adapter with testable idempotent entitlement contracts, a measurable lead-response pilot, and a production operator runbook. These moves improve conversion and trust without enabling an unverified live charge or external side effect.

The highest-return operator moves are to complete one real sandbox OAuth consent/revoke test, deploy the verified archive to the named VPS, connect a real MySQL/TiDB database, configure Nginx/TLS, collect authenticated field timings for every critical module, and onboard one licensed payment gateway. Each should produce evidence, not only a configuration screenshot.

## Value metrics

The product should measure time-to-first-valid-response, qualified-lead precision, missing-field rate, human-edit rate, approval-to-send rate, meeting-request completion, duplicate prevention, provider failure rate, cost per successful work packet, support resolution time, and retention/deletion evidence. Financial value should be calculated from observed outcomes and cost, not from a guaranteed-income claim or a speculative valuation.

## Institutional and ownership boundary

The DSAI brand and product may be operated by the user or a legal entity they control, subject to applicable registration, tax, domain, repository, provider and third-party rights. Current package metadata includes MIT licensing; an owner/legal review is required before describing the whole codebase as exclusive proprietary software. AI assistance does not replace human ownership records, contributor agreements, entity records, trademark decisions or dependency notices.

## Gate policy

A feature may be labeled **Implemented** only when code, test and behavior evidence exist. It may be labeled **Preview** when the user flow is real but side effects are disabled. It may be labeled **Provider-gated** when credentials, contracts or consent are missing. It may be labeled **Operator-gated** when VPS, DNS, database, legal or business action is required. It must not be labeled live merely because a screen or mock API exists.

## Current verdict

DSAI is strongest as a trustworthy Bengali-first workflow and evidence platform with a credible path to lead-response, integration and creative-service products. The practical next launch is a controlled pilot with transparent plans, human approval, read-only integrations, measured response planning and operator-managed deployment. Live payments, autonomous outreach, write-capable integrations and unrestricted media generation should remain closed until their external evidence gates are satisfied.
