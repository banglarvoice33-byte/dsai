# GENESIS Strategy Research Sources

**Reference date:** 15 August 2026

## Competitive pricing and product depth

- Adobe Firefly plan comparison: https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly/plans.html. The page lists a free entry path and paid plans such as Firefly Standard at US$9.99/month, with generative credits and access to image, video, audio, and multiple model providers.
- Runway pricing: https://runwayml.com/pricing. The page lists a free plan with one-time credits and annual-billing prices shown at US$12, US$28, and US$76/month for Standard, Pro, and Max, with explicit image/video/audio credit economics.
- Midjourney plan comparison: https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870484040333-Comparing-Midjourney-Plans. The page lists Basic, Standard, Pro, and Mega at US$10, US$30, US$60, and US$120/month, including Relax-mode capacity on higher tiers.

## Buyer-pain and market evidence

- U.S. Department of Commerce, Bangladesh Digital Economy: https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/bangladesh-digital-economy. Published 19 March 2026. The guide reports 133.61 million internet subscribers as of June 2025, describes Bangladesh as a major online-labor and IT-enabled-services hub, notes that many local firms face limited IT resources and infrastructure when scaling, and identifies digital-skills, security, and data-protection concerns as adoption constraints.
- UNDP Bangladesh, Bridging the Digital Divide: https://www.undp.org/bangladesh/publications/bridging-digital-divide-advancing-accessibility-persons-disabilities-bangladeshs-digital-ecosystem. Published 21 May 2026. The policy brief identifies limited assistive technology, low digital literacy, inaccessible online services, gender inequality, and weak policy implementation as barriers to inclusive digital access.
- SME Foundation / IJSMED, The Road to Digital Maturity: https://ijsmed.smef.gov.bd/article/view/49. Published 1 December 2025. The study reports survey and interview evidence from Dhaka SMEs and identifies affordability constraints, limited digital skills, and regulatory concerns as barriers to ICT adoption; it also reports a positive relationship between ICT adoption and business performance. Treat the study as useful directional evidence and validate its sample and measures with design-partner interviews.
- Digital Health, The impact of limited access to digital health records: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11423383/. Published 23 September 2024. The study surveyed 105 Bangladeshi doctors; it reports substantial reliance on paper-based records, challenges in accessing past patient history, and strong interest in a comprehensive web-based history system. This supports a narrow healthcare communication/document workflow opportunity, not automated diagnosis.

## Safety and platform policy

- Google Play blockchain-based content policy: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13607354?hl=en-GB. It states that apps may not mine cryptocurrency on devices, while remote management is treated differently; tokenized-asset earning requires disclosures and cannot be promoted as guaranteed earnings.
- Bangladesh Bank FE Circular No. 24: https://www.bb.org.bd/mediaroom/circulars/fepd/sep152022fepd24e.pdf. Dated 15 September 2022. It states that transactions for obtaining virtual assets or virtual currencies in, from, or to Bangladesh are not permitted and that facilitating exchange, transfer, or trading is not permitted.
- Apple App Review Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/. The guidelines are a primary platform-policy source for app safety, user-generated content, backend availability, and prohibited background behavior; review the latest version again before any mobile distribution.

## Lawful compute monetization

- Vast.ai GPU hosting: https://vast.ai/hosting. The platform describes a model where hosts list hardware, set prices and terms, and receive platform-supported customer acquisition, billing, scheduling, and support. Revenue is not guaranteed; power, maintenance, bandwidth, depreciation, downtime, taxes, and platform fees must be included in unit economics.
- Managed reserved hosting reference used for GENESIS: https://www.manus.im/. The project guidance states that a reserved 1 vCPU/512 MB instance can cost up to approximately US$37.50/month at full utilization, plus metered egress, before the included monthly usage credit. Verify current billing before enabling it.
