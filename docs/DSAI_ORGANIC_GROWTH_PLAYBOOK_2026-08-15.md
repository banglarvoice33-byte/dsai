# DSAI Organic Growth Playbook

## Operating principle

DSAI should use AI to make research, drafting, Bengali quality review, internal linking, analytics interpretation and experimentation faster. It should not use AI to manufacture reviews, fake users, buy engagement, publish large volumes of near-duplicate pages, impersonate people, or automate unsolicited outreach. Google’s current guidance says generative-AI visibility still depends on foundational SEO, helpful people-first content, crawlability, clear internal links, good page experience, visible text, and structured data that matches the page.[1] It also warns against scaled content abuse and says there are no special AI-search files or hacks required.[1] [2]

## Content system

The first content cluster should answer real Bengali and bilingual questions that map to DSAI’s implemented workflows. Each article should have one primary question, a direct answer near the top, an example or workflow diagram, a clear limitation section, an editorial owner, a last-reviewed date, and one call to action. The content calendar should prioritize “how to use DSAI,” document-to-checklist preparation, Bengali/English workflow friction, API-key safety, one-command deployment, human review, and provider-gated capability explanations.

AI may propose outlines, related questions, translation drafts, metadata and internal links. A human editor must verify Bengali meaning, cultural references, technical commands, legal/medical caution, source links, and whether each claim is implemented in the current release. Do not publish AI-drafted factual claims without source review.

| Content type | Frequency after launch | Success signal |
|---|---:|---|
| Evergreen Bengali Learn guide | 2 per month | Search impressions, engaged time and CTA completion |
| Workflow case study | 1 per month after consented pilots | Qualified demo or pilot request |
| Capability/limit update | Whenever product state changes | Lower confusion and support questions |
| Bengali glossary/evaluation note | 1 per month | Correction rate and repeat use |
| Partner/community resource | 1 per month | Referral traffic and qualified conversations |

## Search and AI-search execution

Verify the production domain in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, submit the sitemap, inspect `/` and `/learn`, and review indexing errors. Google states that pages must be indexed and eligible for normal Search to be eligible as supporting links in AI features; inclusion is never guaranteed.[2] Bing’s AI Performance preview can show citation counts, cited pages and grounding queries across supported Copilot and AI-generated answer surfaces.[3]

Maintain a small number of strong public pages instead of creating one page for every keyword variation. Keep visible text, title, description, headings, FAQ, organization data, contact details, sitemap and robots controls consistent. Structured data must describe information that is visible to users; use the Rich Results Test and Search Console after deployment.[4]

## Analytics and experimentation

The minimum measurement layer should connect Search Console, privacy-conscious site analytics and application events. Track landing page, Learn page, CTA click, sign-in start, successful protected workflow, draft completion, creative request, support contact, pilot request, and returning user. Do not log document contents, API keys or sensitive user data in analytics.

| Funnel stage | Metric | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Impressions, queries, indexed pages, AI citations | Improve topic/structure when impressions exist but qualified clicks remain low |
| Understanding | Learn-page scroll, engaged time, FAQ interaction | Rewrite confusing sections when users leave before the workflow explanation |
| Activation | Sign-in start and completed UAA/Creative action | Fix friction before adding more features |
| Value | Time-to-useful-draft, correction rate, repeat case | Continue only if users report measurable improvement |
| Commercial | Pilot request, paid conversion, renewal, support cost | Do not scale acquisition until retention and margin are visible |

Use monthly before/after comparisons rather than claiming instant SEO growth. Search and AI citations fluctuate, indexing takes time, and traffic alone is not product-market fit.

## Partnership and distribution system

The most credible organic distribution is through people and organizations that already serve Bengali-speaking users: community organizations, migrant-support groups, educational counselors, Bengali professional associations, small agencies, diaspora business networks, research groups and responsible language-technology communities. Offer a co-designed workflow, not a generic referral link. Obtain permission before publishing a partner logo, quote, case study or user story.

Build a simple partner kit containing a one-page explanation, a Bengali demo script, safety and privacy boundaries, a pilot intake form, and a measurement plan. Track qualified referrals and completed workflows, not vanity reach. The site should publish official social accounts only after accounts are created, secured and verified; until then, the current “Coming soon” placeholders are the truthful state.

## AI-assisted operating loop

Once per week, the operator reviews Search Console queries, Bing AI citations where available, Learn-page behavior, support questions and pilot feedback. AI can cluster the inputs into questions, identify missing explanations, propose a content brief and flag contradictory copy. The human owner approves the brief, verifies sources and creates a dated implementation task. Once per month, the team reviews content quality, Bengali corrections, conversion, support load and safety incidents.

A content item is publishable only when its claims match the current product, sources are linked, the Bengali has been reviewed by a competent editor, the CTA leads to a working route, and the item has a planned review date. If a feature is disabled or provider-gated, say so directly.

## 90-day execution plan

| Period | Focus | Concrete output |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–14 | Instrumentation and indexing | Production Search Console/Bing verification, sitemap submission, event taxonomy, privacy review |
| Days 15–30 | Content quality | Four Bengali-first Learn guides, one capability matrix, one safety page, editorial QA checklist |
| Days 31–60 | Proof of value | 20–30 interviews, 3–5 supervised pilots, one consented case study, correction/time metrics |
| Days 61–90 | Conversion and partnerships | Paid pilot offer, partner kit, two community partnerships, retention and margin dashboard |
