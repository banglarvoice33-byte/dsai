# DSAI Launch Research Notes

## Evidence reviewed

Google Search Central recommends people-first content that provides original, complete and satisfying value, clearly communicates who created it, how automation was used when relevant, and why the content exists [1]. This means DSAI promotion should lead with useful Bengali workflows, transparent method pages and real evidence—not mass-produced SEO pages or exaggerated ranking claims.

The FTC’s influencer and review guidance emphasizes truthful endorsements, clear disclosure of material connections, and avoiding fake or paid-for positive reviews [2]. DSAI’s referral system should therefore use explicit Bengali disclosure, immutable referral attribution, payout holds, refund/chargeback adjustment, abuse checks and no fabricated testimonials.

NIST’s AI Risk Management Framework organizes trustworthy AI around Govern, Map, Measure and Manage [3]. Launch promotion should be treated as part of the trust system: claims, case studies, lead capture, consent, audience targeting and incident response need owners and evidence.

## Highest-value launch strategy

The safest launch is a controlled alpha with one sharp promise: Bengali-first accountable work packets for complex everyday work. The funnel should be Education → Proof → Workspace trial → Completed packet → Human review → Export or next action → Consent-based feedback. The first campaigns should target narrow student and professional workflows rather than all audiences simultaneously.

The campaign should use Bengali utility content, short product demonstrations, founder/operator explanations, public evidence updates, partner workshops, community pilots and transparent changelog posts. Paid promotion should begin only after baseline completion, retention, support load and contribution margin are measured.
